01 / STORYWhy we choose ember over flame

Fire that
remembers
the wood.

✺

In 2019 we built a roaster from reclaimed kiln bricks in a shed outside Pune. No gas. Only seasoned jackfruit wood and a thermometer that often failed.

Flame scorches. Ember coaxes. At 187°C the bean cracks for the first time — not with violence, but with a sigh. We listen. We wait.

Every batch is 6 kilos. 22 minutes. One song on vinyl, side A to side B. That’s our measure.
